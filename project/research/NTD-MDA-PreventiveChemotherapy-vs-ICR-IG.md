# NTD MDA / Preventive Chemotherapy vs ICR FHIR IG — Alignment & Gap Analysis

These sources describe **NTD mass drug administration (MDA) / preventive chemotherapy (PC)** — the WHO/AFRO **ESPEN** programme and database ([espen.afro.who.int/node/8](https://espen.afro.who.int/node/8)), the **community drug distributor (CDD) / community-directed treatment with ivermectin (CDTI)** delivery model ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)), the **ESPEN database / district-level endemicity & coverage reporting** and its data-quality challenges ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)), MDA-as-UHC with **community-level treatment registers** in Liberia ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)), **microplanning** for LF MDA ([PLoS NTD 10.1371/journal.pntd.0012105](https://journals.plos.org/plosntds/article?id=10.1371/journal.pntd.0012105)), and the **NTD Toolbox** MDA delivery-platform guidance ([NTD Toolbox / GSA](https://www.ntdtoolbox.org/COVID19MDA)). Compared against the **ICR FHIR R4 Implementation Guide** (publisher UNICEF; Ona + Crosscut), **IG v0.1.0 / explainer v0.7.0**.

This is the archetype the three vaccine-SIA analyses (WHO-SIA-2016, WHO-AFRO-Measles, GTFCC-OCV) explicitly **could not validate**: the **community-directed / household drug-treatment** half of the IG (`campaign-type = mda`, `delivery-strategy = community-directed`, `ICRMedicationAdministration`, `group-kind = community`). It is tested hard here.

> **Sourcing note.** The NTD Toolbox primary URL ([eliminateschisto.org/resources/ntd-toolbox-resource-practical-who-guidance](https://www.eliminateschisto.org/resources/ntd-toolbox-resource-practical-who-guidance)) returned a hard **HTTP 403** via WebFetch; its content (MDA delivery platforms: school-based vs community-based door-to-door/household vs central) was recovered from the equivalent NTD Toolbox primary page ([ntdtoolbox.org/COVID19MDA](https://www.ntdtoolbox.org/COVID19MDA)) and the GSA/PubMed delivery-mode systematic review ([PMID 29077723](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29077723/)). The WHO equity guidance landing page ([WHO/FWC/17.3](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-FWC-17.3)) is a metadata stub (the 94-page PDF is not machine-fetchable); the three-coverage and 65%-threshold definitions are grounded instead in WHO GPELF materials, the ENVISION drug-coverage monitoring resource, and peer-reviewed sources, all cited inline.

## 1. Executive summary

The ICR IG anticipated MDA more deliberately than it anticipated any other non-vaccine archetype: it has a dedicated `mda` campaign-type, a `community-directed` delivery strategy, a `community` group-kind on `ICRDeliveryUnit`, an `ICRMedicationAdministration` event whose `subject` can be a `Patient` **or** an `ICRDeliveryUnit` (so register-level / community capture is allowed), a `directlyObserved` element for swallowed-vs-handed-out, and an MDA medication value set bound to **WHO ATC** codes (albendazole P02CA03, ivermectin P02CA01, praziquantel P02BA01, azithromycin J01FA10, DEC P02CB02). Against the ESPEN/CDTI reality, the **skeleton fits**: school-based and community-directed delivery, the CDD-compiled community register aggregating to implementation-unit (IU) summaries, integrated multi-drug rounds, and the ATC-coded donated medicines all have a home in the existing profiles ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/); [Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)).

The **dominant gap is the three coverage types**, which is the defining feature of PC-NTD M&E and which the IG's `administrative` vs `survey`/`lqas`/`rcm` axis does **not** capture. NTD programmes distinguish: **programme (reported) coverage** = treated ÷ total population enumerated/targeted; **epidemiological coverage** = treated ÷ the disease-eligible at-risk population (the elimination-relevant metric, e.g. LF's **≥65% sustained** threshold); and **geographic coverage** = proportion of endemic IUs that actually conducted MDA ([WHO equity guidance WHO/FWC/17.3](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-FWC-17.3); [ENVISION drug-coverage monitoring](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy); [WHO GPELF](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/lymphatic-filariasis/global-programme-to-eliminate-lymphatic-filariasis)). These have **different denominators and different decision-meanings**, and the IG's design invariant that "three coverage lineages [administrative/survey/independent] are never merged" is the *wrong axis*: for MDA the load-bearing distinction is the **denominator** (total vs at-risk) and the **unit** (people vs IUs), not the data source. This is the single highest-value addition.

Three further MDA-specific gaps cluster behind it. (a) **Coverage targets / thresholds** are absent — the IG has no element to store "LF elimination needs ≥65% epidemiological coverage" or oncho's effective-coverage target, even though programmes trigger corrective mop-up and TAS decisions off them. (b) The **community treatment register grain** — per individual/household, by drug × age × sex, with the directly-observed flag — sits in tension with the IG's **one-Task-per-visit aggregate** model: the register *can* be expressed as many `ICRMedicationAdministration` resources, but the IG has no **CDD performer role** and no native individual-by-drug-by-age/sex tally structure, so most programmes will land at the aggregate IU level the ESPEN database itself uses. (c) **Endemicity / mapping / TAS / impact surveys** decide *whether and where* MDA runs — they are the upstream gate on geographic coverage — and the IG has no endemicity-status or survey-decision representation at all.

Net: the IG is **adequate as a container** for an MDA round and is clearly the best-fitted of the campaign types for this archetype, but it is **not yet sufficient** to satisfy ESPEN/WHO PC reporting. The decisive additions are a **coverage-type axis (programme / epidemiological / geographic)** with an explicit **epidemiological at-risk denominator**, a **coverage-target element**, a **CDD distributor role**, and an **endemicity/disease-status** representation. Of these, the coverage-type distinction and CDD role are genuinely MDA-specific; coverage-target storage and the denominator-provenance work are general and corroborate prior sibling analyses.

## 2. Where the document ALIGNS with the IG

- **`mda` campaign-type exists and is correct.** The five PC-NTDs (LF, onchocerciasis, schistosomiasis, STH, trachoma) treated by MDA/preventive chemotherapy ([ESPEN](https://espen.afro.who.int/node/8); [NTD Toolbox](https://www.ntdtoolbox.org/COVID19MDA)) map directly onto `campaign-type = mda`. Unlike OCV (which had to borrow `vaccination-sia`), MDA has a purpose-built type.
- **Community-directed delivery is first-class.** CDTI — "villagers selected by their communities to … distribute ivermectin," originating with APOC ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)) — maps to `delivery-strategy = community-directed`. School-based and house-to-house/household delivery ([NTD Toolbox](https://www.ntdtoolbox.org/COVID19MDA); [PMID 29077723](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29077723/)) map to `delivery-strategy = school` and `house-to-house`. Central-in-community distribution maps to `fixed-post`/`temporary-post` (`location-type = community-distribution-point`). The full PC delivery-platform taxonomy is representable.
- **Community as a delivery unit.** The community-level enumeration/treatment unit ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)) maps to `ICRDeliveryUnit` (Group actual=true) with `group-kind = community` (and `household` / `school-cohort` for the other platforms), with geography via `group-location`. The Liberia "3,195 community distribution points" model fits `location-type = community-distribution-point`.
- **Drug events with ATC + directly-observed.** CDD registers record "number of eligible individuals receiving ivermectin and albendazole, disaggregated by sex and age" ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)); ivermectin/albendazole/praziquantel/azithromycin/DEC are exactly the IG's ATC-bound MDA value set. `ICRMedicationAdministration` carries the drug, and its `directlyObserved` element captures swallowed-vs-handed-out — which matters for compliance-based coverage in CDTI ([PMC8751475](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8751475/)).
- **Register-level / community-subject capture is already permitted.** Because `ICRMedicationAdministration.subject` may be `Patient` **or** `ICRDeliveryUnit`, the IG already supports aggregate community capture — the mode the ESPEN database actually uses (IU-level totals), not only per-patient capture. This is a deliberate and well-judged design choice for this archetype.
- **Denominator with provenance.** ESPEN coverage = treated ÷ "target population offered treatment," with the well-known denominator problems (coverage >100%, 2–3% annual growth, census staleness) ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)). `ICRTargetPopulation` (Group actual=false) + `denominator-source` (`census`/`census-projection`/`microcensus`/`worldpop`/`grid3`/`hmis`) + denominator-date + `planning-denominator` is precisely the provenance scaffolding these data-quality critiques call for.
- **Integrated multi-drug MDA.** Co-delivering multiple PC-NTD drugs in one round (e.g. ivermectin + albendazole for LF/oncho; integrated national PC programmes) ([PMC3005506](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3005506/)) maps to `campaign-type = integrated` with multiple `ICRMedicationAdministration` resources per visit.
- **Implementation unit = operational geography.** ESPEN's reporting unit is the "health implementation unit (IU) … administrative divisions used for organizing and implementing health interventions" ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)). `ICRLocation` (admin `partOf` + operational geography incl. supervisory-area/operational-area + GERS/P-code) accommodates IU and sub-IU, and the microplanning "supervision area boundaries by GPS" ([PLoS NTD pntd.0012105](https://journals.plos.org/plosntds/article?id=10.1371/journal.pntd.0012105)) maps to `location-type = supervisory-area`.
- **Microplanning / hard-to-reach.** The microplanning workflow (GPS post inventory, supervision-area boundaries, eligibility, safety-driven non-coverage) ([PLoS NTD pntd.0012105](https://journals.plos.org/plosntds/article?id=10.1371/journal.pntd.0012105)) aligns with the IG's microplanning posture and `missed-reason`/`noncompliance-reason` axes (`absent`, `refusal`, `inaccessible`, `safety-concern`) — corroborating the RED-microplanning sibling analysis.

## 3. Gaps & divergences

### 3a. Things the document requires that the IG does NOT yet represent

1. **The three coverage types (programme / epidemiological / geographic) — REAL GAP, MDA-specific, highest value.** PC-NTD M&E rests on three distinct coverage metrics with **different denominators**: **programme (reported) coverage** = treated ÷ total population enumerated/targeted (includes ineligibles — under-5s, pregnant, breastfeeding, severely ill); **epidemiological coverage** = treated ÷ the disease-eligible *at-risk* population (the elimination metric); **geographic coverage** = proportion of endemic IUs that conducted MDA ([WHO/FWC/17.3](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-FWC-17.3); [ENVISION](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy)). Liberia computed programme coverage "over the total population enumerated by the CDD … including ineligible populations," then separately checked the **65% epidemiologic threshold** (11/15 counties met it for oncho; 9/13 for LF) ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)). The IG's coverage axis is `administrative` vs `survey`/`lqas`/`rcm` — a **data-source** axis. It has **no coverage-type axis** and **no separate at-risk/eligible epidemiological denominator** distinct from the total-population denominator. This is the defining MDA M&E requirement the IG does not meet. (Partly an instance of the already-acknowledged "Measure definitions aligned to WHO/ESPEN minimums" gap — but the *denominator/coverage-type axis* is a new structural requirement, not just a missing Measure.)

2. **Coverage targets / thresholds as a stored element — REAL GAP (general, corroborated).** LF elimination requires **≥65% epidemiological coverage of the total at-risk population, sustained for ≥5 annual rounds**, before TAS ([WHO GPELF](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/lymphatic-filariasis/global-programme-to-eliminate-lymphatic-filariasis); oncho effective coverage ≥80% over 12–15 yrs, [PMC8751475](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8751475/)). Programmes trigger corrective mop-up and stop/continue decisions off these targets, and "corrective action protocols when coverage targets are unmet" ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)). The IG has **no coverage-target element** on `ICRCampaignProtocol`/`ICRCampaignActivity`. Already flagged by sibling analyses (coverage-target storage); MDA gives it concrete disease-specific thresholds.

3. **Endemicity status + TAS/impact-survey decision gate — REAL GAP, MDA-specific.** Whether and where MDA runs is decided by **endemicity mapping** (prevalence categories high >50% / moderate 20–50% / low <20%) and by **TAS/impact surveys** that decide stop/continue ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/); [WHO GPELF / TAS](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/lymphatic-filariasis/global-programme-to-eliminate-lymphatic-filariasis)). This upstream gate *defines geographic coverage* (denominator = endemic IUs). The IG has **no endemicity/disease-status representation** and **no survey-decision (TAS/impact) artifact**. `ICRSurveyCoverage` is a coverage MeasureReport, not an endemicity/transmission-assessment record.

4. **CDD as a distinct performer / role — REAL GAP, MDA-specific.** The **community drug distributor** is the defining actor: census, drug-quantity calculation, dosing/exclusion enforcement, register completion, adverse-event referral ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)); the "unsung foot soldiers" of MDA ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)). The IG has no CDD/community-distributor role code for `performer`/`practitioner-role`. Without it, the register's authorship (and CDD performance monitoring, supervision checklists) cannot be expressed distinctly from facility-based providers.

5. **Eligibility exclusions + dose-pole (height-based) dosing — REAL GAP, MDA-specific.** Ivermectin MDA excludes children <90 cm / <15 kg (~under 5), pregnant and (often) breastfeeding women, and the severely ill, and doses by **height via a dose pole** (90–119 cm = 1 tab; 120–139 = 2; 140–159 = 3; >159 = 4) ([dose-pole / APOC guidance](https://countdown.lstmed.ac.uk/sites/default/files/centre/How%20to%20give%20out%20the%20medicines-%20Ivermectin%20and%20Albendazole.pdf); [Mundo Sano](https://mundosano.org/en/a-simpler-ivermectin-dosing-strategy-can-transform-global-parasite-control/)). The IG has `eligible-present`/`eligible-absent` and `missed-reason`/`noncompliance-reason`, but **no eligibility-exclusion reason set** (pregnancy / too-young-or-short / recent-treatment) and **no height/dose-pole dosing** representation. These exclusions are exactly what makes programme coverage (incl. ineligibles in denominator) differ from epidemiological coverage.

6. **Geographic-coverage measure (endemic-IU denominator) — REAL GAP, MDA-specific.** Geographic coverage = endemic IUs that conducted MDA ÷ all endemic IUs ([ENVISION](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy)). This is a **measure over IUs, not over people** — a different population type than any current `MeasureReport`. No IG Measure expresses it.

### 3b. Things the IG models that the document treats differently (or contradicts)

1. **Coverage axis: data-source (IG) vs denominator/unit (MDA) — MODELLING TENSION.** The IG invariant "three coverage lineages never merged" means `administrative` / `survey` / `lqas` / `rcm`. For MDA the never-merge boundary that actually matters is **programme vs epidemiological vs geographic** (denominator and unit), which is *orthogonal* to data source — programme coverage is itself administrative; epidemiological coverage can be administrative *or* survey-derived. The IG's axis is not wrong, but it is **insufficient**: MDA needs a second, orthogonal coverage-type axis. (Real gap, framed as a modelling tension because the existing axis stays.)

2. **One-Task-per-visit aggregate vs the community treatment register grain — MODELLING TENSION.** The CDD register is intrinsically **per individual/household, by drug × age × sex, with a directly-observed flag** ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139); [PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)). The IG's one-Task-per-visit model targets the *visit/community*, not the register row. The register *can* be modelled as N `ICRMedicationAdministration` resources (subject = Patient for per-row, or = `ICRDeliveryUnit` for the IU tally), so this is not a hard contradiction — but the IG has no native age×sex×drug tally structure and no register/Task-row identity, so in practice most data will land at the **aggregate IU level the ESPEN database itself uses** ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)). Acceptable for ESPEN reporting; lossy for individual-compliance/TAS-grade analysis. (Modelling choice with a documented limitation.)

3. **`record-origin` firewall vs ESPEN's ministry-only/duplicate-source problem.** ESPEN captures Federal-MoH MDA only, **excludes school-based and philanthropic deworming**, and risks **duplicate reporting** when multiple orgs treat overlapping regions ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)). The IG's `campaign`/`routine` firewall doesn't capture **reporting-source / implementing-organization**, so it cannot natively express the school-vs-MoH-vs-NGO provenance distinction that drives ESPEN's completeness gaps. (New MDA-flavoured provenance requirement, adjacent to but not the same as record-origin.)

## 5. Proposed terminology additions (flag for the IG)

1. **`coverage-type` axis — ADD a CodeSystem `{programme, epidemiological, geographic}`** as a *required* dimension on `ICRAdministrativeCoverage` / `ICRSurveyCoverage` MeasureReports, **orthogonal** to `coverage-source`. Justification: programme vs epidemiological vs geographic have different denominators and decision-meanings and must never be conflated ([WHO/FWC/17.3](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-FWC-17.3); [ENVISION](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy); [Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)). This is the top recommendation.

2. **Epidemiological (at-risk/eligible) denominator — ADD a second `ICRTargetPopulation` flavour or a `denominator-population-type` extension `{total-population, at-risk-eligible}`.** Justification: epidemiological coverage's denominator excludes ineligibles; without a distinct at-risk Group the IG cannot compute the elimination metric ([Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139)).

3. **Coverage-target element — ADD `coverage-target` (value + coverage-type + disease) on `ICRCampaignProtocol`/`ICRCampaignActivity`.** Justification: LF ≥65% epidemiological sustained ≥5 rounds; oncho ≥80% effective ([WHO GPELF](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/lymphatic-filariasis/global-programme-to-eliminate-lymphatic-filariasis); [PMC8751475](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8751475/)). Triggers mop-up / TAS.

4. **CDD / community-distributor role — ADD a code to the `performer`/`practitioner-role` value set (e.g. `community-drug-distributor`).** Justification: the defining MDA actor and register author ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/)). Enables CDD-performance supervision.

5. **Eligibility-exclusion reason set — ADD a CodeSystem `{pregnancy, breastfeeding, age-or-height-too-low, severely-ill, recent-treatment, other}`** attached to `eligible-absent`. Justification: explains programme-vs-epidemiological denominator difference and dosing safety ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/); [dose-pole guidance](https://mundosano.org/en/a-simpler-ivermectin-dosing-strategy-can-transform-global-parasite-control/)).

6. **Endemicity / disease-status — ADD a CodeSystem `{endemic-high, endemic-moderate, endemic-low, non-endemic, under-surveillance, post-MDA-surveillance}`** on `ICRLocation` (per disease). Justification: ESPEN's categorical prevalence and the geographic-coverage denominator ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/)).

7. **Height / dose-pole dosing — ADD a height (cm) element or a `dose-band` extension on `ICRMedicationAdministration`.** Justification: ivermectin tablets-by-height is the field dosing method ([dose-pole / APOC](https://countdown.lstmed.ac.uk/sites/default/files/centre/How%20to%20give%20out%20the%20medicines-%20Ivermectin%20and%20Albendazole.pdf)).

8. **Geographic-coverage Measure — ADD a `Measure` whose population is endemic IUs (`ICRLocation`), not Patients.** Justification: geographic coverage is computed over units ([ENVISION](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy)).

## 7. Use cases not yet identified in the IG

1. **A CDTI round recorded in a community register by a CDD.** A named CDD enumerates the community, treats by dose-pole height, marks directly-observed swallowing, records age×sex×drug, refers an adverse event. *Resources:* `ICRMedicationAdministration` (per row or IU tally) + a **CDD `performer` role** (missing) + **dose-pole height** (missing) + **AE referral** (acknowledged gap). ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/); [Int Health 16(3):283](https://academic.oup.com/inthealth/article/16/3/283/7164139))
2. **Computing programme vs epidemiological vs geographic coverage for an IU.** Three numbers, three denominators (total enumerated; at-risk eligible; endemic-IUs-treated ÷ endemic-IUs). *Resources:* `ICRAdministrativeCoverage`/`ICRSurveyCoverage` + **coverage-type axis** (missing) + **at-risk denominator** (missing) + **geographic-coverage Measure over IUs** (missing). ([WHO/FWC/17.3](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-FWC-17.3); [ENVISION](https://www.ntdenvision.org/resource/who_ntd_guidelines_and_reports/monitoring_drug_coverage_for_preventive_chemotherapy))
3. **Integrated multi-NTD MDA on one population.** One round co-delivers ivermectin + albendazole (+ praziquantel) to a community. *Resources:* `campaign-type = integrated` + multiple `ICRMedicationAdministration` (supported today). ([PMC3005506](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3005506/))
4. **Eligibility screening + dose-pole dosing.** Screen out pregnant/under-90 cm/recently-treated; dose remainder by height band. *Resources:* `eligible-absent` + **exclusion-reason set** (missing) + **height/dose-band** (missing). ([dose-pole / APOC](https://countdown.lstmed.ac.uk/sites/default/files/centre/How%20to%20give%20out%20the%20medicines-%20Ivermectin%20and%20Albendazole.pdf))
5. **Endemicity mapping / TAS deciding whether & where MDA runs.** Prevalence mapping sets endemic IUs; TAS/impact survey decides stop/continue. *Resources:* **endemicity status on `ICRLocation`** (missing) + a **TAS/impact-survey artifact** (missing). ([PMC12212196](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212196/); [WHO GPELF/TAS](https://www.who.int/teams/control-of-neglected-tropical-diseases/lymphatic-filariasis/global-programme-to-eliminate-lymphatic-filariasis))
6. **CDD-performance / supervision monitoring.** Supervision checklists tracking CDD coverage, register completeness, attrition. *Resources:* needs **CDD role** + supervision representation (sibling-flagged supervision gap). ([PMC6790237](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6790237/))

## 8. Bottom line

The IG is the **best-fitted of all campaign types for NTD MDA** — it is the only archetype with a dedicated campaign-type, a community-directed strategy, a community group-kind, an ATC-coded drug value set, a directly-observed flag, and a medication event that already permits register-level/community-subject capture. As a **container for an MDA round**, it is adequate, and it validates the design choices (delivery strategy first-class, denominator-with-provenance, register-level capture) against real ESPEN/CDTI practice. But it is **not yet sufficient for WHO/ESPEN PC reporting**, because the metric that defines this archetype — the three coverage types — sits on the wrong axis.

**Top recommended IG changes (in priority order):**
1. **Add a `coverage-type` axis (`programme` / `epidemiological` / `geographic`)** orthogonal to `coverage-source`, plus an **at-risk/eligible epidemiological denominator** distinct from the total-population denominator. *(MDA-specific, decisive.)*
2. **Add a coverage-target element** carrying disease-specific thresholds (LF ≥65% epidemiological sustained ≥5 rounds; oncho ≥80% effective). *(General, corroborates sibling analyses.)*
3. **Add a CDD / community-distributor performer role**, and an **eligibility-exclusion reason set** + **dose-pole/height dosing** on the MDA event. *(MDA-specific.)*
4. **Add an endemicity/disease-status code on `ICRLocation`** and a **geographic-coverage Measure computed over endemic IUs**, plus a **TAS/impact-survey decision artifact**. *(MDA-specific.)*
5. **Add a reporting-source / implementing-org axis** to address ESPEN's ministry-only/duplicate-source completeness problem. *(MDA-flavoured; consider.)*

**MDA-specific vs general.** Genuinely MDA-specific: the three coverage types, the at-risk denominator, the CDD role, eligibility-exclusion + dose-pole, endemicity/TAS, geographic coverage. General (recur across archetypes, already flagged by siblings): coverage-target storage, denominator provenance, supervision, AEFI, and the "Measure definitions aligned to WHO/ESPEN minimums" the IG already lists as an open gap. The community-register-grain-vs-one-Task-per-visit tension is a **modelling choice** the IG can keep (aggregate IU capture matches what ESPEN itself stores), provided it documents the loss of individual-compliance/TAS-grade granularity.
