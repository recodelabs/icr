Diagram options: beyond campaigns

Three ways to show that the place a campaign records is the same place every other programme uses. All three replace the current prose in the "beyond campaigns" section. Built with the site's palette and type so any of them can drop straight in.

Option 1 · static
Beyond campaigns

Same household. Same facility. Same record, whichever programme arrives.

The simplest to read and the closest to how you said it. Two programmes, two starting points, one door, one ID. Works at any width and needs no interaction.

Option 2 · interactive
Beyond campaigns

Two programmes, one map. Flip the lens and nothing underneath moves.

Base layer: boundaries, facilities, households (never changes) Campaign overlay Routine overlay The same household in both

The reader does the comparison themselves. The base layer, boundaries, facilities, and households, is identical in both views; only the programme overlay changes. Reuses the hero map's visual language. More work to maintain, and it needs a static fallback for print or slides.

Option 3 · static
Beyond campaigns

Places and people are drawn once. Every programme is a layer on top.

The FHIR argument as a picture: one base of places and people, programmes stacked above and pointing down into it. Clean and complete, but it is the diagram every data platform draws, and it is the least concrete of the three for a field or ministry reader.
