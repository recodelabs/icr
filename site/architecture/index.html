Integrated Campaign Registry

ICR solution architecture

The ICR holds

  • The location registry: admin areas, facilities, catchments, settlements
  • Campaigns and rounds, targets and results
  • Shared codes, and how each tool's codes map to them

Other systems keep doing

  • Data collection in CommCare, DIGIT, ODK and DHIS2 Tracker
  • Microplanning in Crosscut
  • Routine reporting in DHIS2, the national HMIS

Example: what goes in and out of the ICR in one country's NTD programme

Sightsavers runs the NTD programme with Crosscut for planning, CommCare for collection and Metabase for its dashboards. Other programmes in the same country use ICF and DIGIT. The ICR gives all of them the same locations and populations, and collects their results in one place.
